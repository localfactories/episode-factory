Checked, not hoped

Why it is different

Most voice tools speak and hope. Voice Factory listens back to every sentence and fixes it before you ever hear it.

Every sentence checked

Each part is transcribed and compared with your text word by word. A word missing or wrong is recorded again; an extra word, a stumble or a long gap is cut out. If a part still is not right after five tries, the best take is kept and named in the report.

Any length, no bills

A paragraph or a whole book. The voice, the checks and the subtitles all run on your own graphics card, so there is nothing per character and nothing per minute.

Change one sentence, redo one sentence

Edit a line in the studio and only that part is recorded and checked again. Everything else keeps its checked take.

Five languages, the same check

English, Spanish, Arabic, Chinese and Japanese — each listened to and repaired the same way, numbers read out properly, Arabic shown right to left.

Subtitles in your own words

Every narration comes with subtitles timed to the voice and written in your script's words, not the transcriber's guesses — as SRT and VTT, with the levelled WAV, an MP3 and the text.

Your files stay with you

Nothing you write is sent anywhere. Your projects live in a folder on your PC and are kept even if you uninstall.

Three steps

How it works

One studio window. Every step runs on your machine.

01 · TEXT

Paste it or open a file

Paste text, or drop a file: .txt, .docx, .pdf, .epub, .odt, .html, .rtf, .md, or subtitles (.srt, .vtt) to voice again.

02 · VOICE

Choose the voice

Ten to fifteen seconds of clean speech in the voice you want — your own, or one you have permission to use. Then the language.

03 · MAKE

Recorded, listened to, repaired

Every part is recorded, checked word by word and fixed. Then it is joined, the loudness is levelled, and the subtitles are written.

04 · FILES

Ready to use

WAV, MP3, SRT and VTT subtitles and the text — for a video, a podcast, an audiobook or a course.

Good to know

Questions

Something else? Write to support@local-factories.com.

How does the free trial work?

Press "Download free" and enter your email – no card, no PayPal. The download arrives by email. Install it and open the studio: the 7 days start by themselves on that PC. When they end, the studio shows a Subscribe button; after paying it unlocks by itself.

Whose voice can I use?

Your own, or a voice you have the speaker's permission to use. The studio asks you to confirm this each time you add a voice. Never use a voice to pretend to be someone.

How is it checked?

After each part is recorded, a transcriber on your PC listens to it and the words are compared with your text, one by one. Missing or wrong words are recorded again; extra words, stumbles and long gaps are cut. The report says what was done to each part.

Which languages?

English, Spanish, Arabic, Chinese and Japanese. Each gets the same check; numbers are read out in that language.

What do I get at the end?

A levelled WAV and an MP3 of the whole narration, its subtitles as SRT and VTT in your own words, and the text.

I already have Episode Factory. Do I need Voice Factory?

Episode Factory makes finished films: narration, footage and render. Voice Factory is the narration on its own, for audiobooks, podcasts, courses or videos you edit yourself. They are separate subscriptions; Voice Factory shares Episode Factory's voice engine on the same PC, so it installs in minutes.

Can I cancel?

Any time, from the link in your receipt or the studio's Setup page. Your projects stay on your PC.

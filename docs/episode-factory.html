One press

How it works

One program, one press. Every stage runs on your machine and leaves its evidence in the episode folder.

01 · WRITE

Attach your script

Paste or attach a script of any length. The program splits it into short pieces and plans the pictures from the words themselves — the place, the creatures, the subjects it names.

02 · VOICE

Your voice, verified

A local speech engine narrates in the voice of a ten- to fifteen-second clip you give it. Every piece is transcribed back and checked word by word; what does not match is recorded again.

03 · FOOTAGE

Found and judged

Stock clips are fetched from free libraries for every subject, and a small vision model looks at a frame of each candidate before it is kept: footage that does not match the words is refused.

04 · RENDER

A finished 4K film

The narration is mastered, the montage is cut to the words, and a quality gate refuses a film that is not complete. You get the film, the transcript and the chapter timestamps.

Requirements

What you need

Hardware you own and a free footage key. Nothing inside the program is metered: every film, any length.

Computer
Windows 10 or 11, with an NVIDIA graphics card from 8 GB. The program reads the card and adapts to it: a film was made end to end with the card held to 8 GB, and to 12 GB, and on the full 24 GB. A card of 32 GB or more runs two speech engines at once. The picture judge takes the card when there is room and the processor otherwise.
Disk
About 35 GB for the install (the speech engine and the vision model are fetched from their publishers once) and about 55 GB of working space while an episode renders.
Footage
One free footage key, from Pexels or Pixabay – either one is enough. A Pixabay key is shown as soon as you sign in to a free account.
Voice
Ten to fifteen seconds of clean speech in the voice you want the narration in — yours, or one you have the rights to.
Internet
For the install and for fetching clips. The narration, the transcription and the render never leave your machine.

How does the free trial work?

Press "Download free" on your Windows PC – it downloads straight away, no email, no card. When they end, the program tells you; subscribe from inside it for £19 a month – nothing is reinstalled, and your films and settings are kept. The subscription then renews by itself every month until you cancel. If you don't subscribe, nothing is ever charged.

Is there really no limit?

None. Make as many films as you like, of any length: nothing is counted. The voice and the transcript are made by programs on your own machine, and the clips come from free libraries under their free keys.

Can I cancel?

Any time, from the store's customer portal. The program stops starting new episodes when the paid month ends; your films, your episodes and your settings stay yours.

Where does the footage come from, and may I publish it?

From Pexels and Pixabay – whichever key you add, or both – under their own licences, which allow use in published videos. Put a footage credit in your description; the program's documentation lists the vendors' terms with the dates they were read. Read them yourself before you publish for money — they are the vendors' terms, not ours.

Does it need the internet while it runs?

Yes, for fetching clips and for the short check of your plan each time you press Run. Narration, transcription, judging and rendering are local. The program checks for updates once a day; that check can be switched off.

Is there a refund?

No - that is what the free trial is for: try it for 7 days first. After that a month is paid for and delivered as it runs, and all sales are final; cancel from the portal and the plan simply ends at the renewal. Check the requirements above before you start; if the install does not complete on a machine that meets them, write to us with the install log and we will get it running with you.

Can I move the licence to a new computer?

Yes. The plan runs on one machine at a time. Setting the program up again on the same computer needs nothing – it carries on by itself. To move to a different computer, write to support@local-factories.com and we move it for you.

Which languages?

The speech engine narrates in the language of your script and your voice clip; the product is documented and tested in English.

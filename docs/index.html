Questions

Before you choose

Anything else: support@local-factories.com

Is it one subscription for both apps?

No. Each app is its own plan with its own free week: Episode Factory is £19 a month, Voice Factory is £9 a month. Cancel either at any time.

Which one do I need?

Episode Factory if you want the finished film: the narration, the footage and the 4K render. Voice Factory if you want the narration alone — audiobooks, voice-overs, long videos — with subtitles, in five languages.

Does anything I write leave my PC?

No. Your text, your voices and your finished files stay on your PC. The apps go online to install, to check your plan and, in Episode Factory, to fetch the footage.

Do they run on a Mac?

No. Both need Windows 10 or 11 and an NVIDIA graphics card with 8 GB or more. Not sure what you have? Open Task Manager → Performance → GPU and read “Dedicated GPU memory”.
